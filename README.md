🚰 Smart Water Tank Monitoring & Management System | Arduino Project
As part of the Smart Sense Club Recruitment Process at IIITDM Kancheepuram, I designed and simulated a Smart Water Tank Monitoring & Management System using Arduino Uno in Tinkercad.

🔹 Key Features
📊 Continuously monitors the water level using an HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor.
📟 Displays the real-time water level percentage on a 16×2 LCD.
🚨 If the water level drops below 10%, the buzzer is activated and the water pump (DC motor) automatically turns ON to refill the tank.
💡 A white LED glows whenever the motor is running, providing a clear visual indication of pump operation.
✅ Once the water level reaches 80%, the pump automatically turns OFF to prevent unnecessary operation.
⚠️ If the water level exceeds 90%, the buzzer is activated again to warn about a potential overflow condition.
🟢🟡🔴 LED indicators display the current water level status at a glance.

🎮 IR Remote Controls
1️⃣ Button: Display the Water Level page.
2️⃣ Button: Display the Motor Status page.
3️⃣ Button: Display the System Status page.
 ▲  Up Button: Manually turn the water pump ON.
 ▼ Down Button: Manually turn the water pump OFF.
 ⏻ Power Button: Toggle between Automatic and Manual operating modes.
⏯  Button: Mute the buzzer alarm.

This project enhanced my understanding of embedded systems, sensor interfacing, automation logic, real-time monitoring, and user-controlled embedded applications while developing a practical smart water management solution in Tinkercad.
